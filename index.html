About

FAQ

Common questions

Do you work remotely or on-site?

Both. On-site in Cluj-Napoca and the surrounding region, and remotely for clients across Europe. Audits usually combine a short on-site or video session with remote analysis.

Do you sell software licenses?

No. We're independent, so recommendations are based only on what's best for your team. We're happy to work alongside your existing software reseller.

We're a small team. Is this for us?

Yes. Small teams often benefit the most, because good methods and templates set up early save years of cleanup later. Engagements are scaled to your size.

Is AI safe to use with our confidential CAD data?

It can be, when it's set up correctly. We help you choose tools and configurations that respect your confidentiality and customer requirements, and define clear rules for what data goes where.

How are projects priced?

The workflow audit is a fixed-price package. Larger projects like migrations or training programs are quoted per project after the intro call, so you always know the cost upfront.
