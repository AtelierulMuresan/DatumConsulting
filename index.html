About

Hi, I'm Andrei.

I'm a mechanical design engineer and team lead based in Cluj-Napoca, Romania, with 12 years of experience in CAD, engineering, data management and automation. I've spent that time designing production parts for the automotive industry, from plastic injection molded components to die casting, sheet metal and composites, in CATIA and a range of other CAD platforms.

Leading a team of engineers taught me that most delays aren't caused by hard engineering problems. They come from messy data, inconsistent methods and repetitive manual work. That's what Datum Consulting fixes.

I've been using and integrating AI in engineering work since the first generative AI tools appeared, and I build my own engineering tools in Python and C#. Alongside consulting, I'm pursuing a PhD in additive manufacturing and composites, so I bring both the designer's view and the automation mindset.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you work remotely or on-site?

Both. On-site in Cluj-Napoca and the surrounding region, and remotely for clients across Europe and worldwide. Audits usually combine a short on-site or video session with remote analysis.

Do you sell software licenses?

No. We're independent, so recommendations are based only on what's best for your team. We're happy to work alongside your existing software reseller.

We're a small team. Is this for us?

Yes. Small teams often benefit the most, because good methods and templates set up early save years of cleanup later. Engagements are scaled to your size.

Is AI safe to use with our confidential CAD data?

It can be, when it's set up correctly. We help you choose tools and configurations that respect your confidentiality and customer requirements, and define clear rules for what data goes where.

How are projects priced?

There are no fixed packages. After the discovery call we build a plan around your needs and agree on scope, timeline and cost upfront, so there are no surprises.
